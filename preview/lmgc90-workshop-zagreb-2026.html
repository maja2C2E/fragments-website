LMGC90 Workshop

Modelling masonry structures

When

2–4 December 2026
Wednesday: guest lecture at 10:00
LMGC90 sessions 13:30–17:00
Thursday & Friday: 09:00–17:00

Where

Zagreb, Croatia
University of Zagreb
Faculty of Civil Engineering

Three days of theory and guided practice in LMGC90, with a focus on contact mechanics and the modelling of masonry structures.

The workshop moves from the basic calculation workflow and 2D rigid-body examples to contact mechanics, 3D models and selected masonry applications. Participants will work with existing tutorials and scripts, supported by the organising team.

Lecturer
Frédéric Dubois · CNRS / LMGC, Montpellier

Practical support
Maja Baniček & Mahmoud Shaqfa

Who it is for

Researchers, doctoral students and engineers.

Basic mechanics, introductory Python and Unix terminal skills are recommended. Previous knowledge of Non-Smooth Contact Dynamics (NSCD) or Cohesive Zone Models (CZM) is not required.

Participants should arrive with a working course installation. Software requirements and preparation details will be provided before the workshop.

Three days of training

Preliminary programme

Revised preliminary programme: Wednesday morning includes the Notre-Dame guest lecture. The workshop runs until 17:00 on Friday, with a final afternoon of guided masonry modelling. Minor adjustments may be made by the lecturer.

Notre-Dame guest lecture: details and venue ↗

Breaks: Thursday and Friday 10:45–11:00; daily 15:00–15:15. Lunch: daily 12:30–13:30. Wednesday morning is reserved for the guest lecture and the gathering that follows.

02

Wednesday, 2 December 2026

Guest lecture, LMGC90 workflow & 2D models

10:00

Guest lecture: restoration of Notre-Dame Cathedral

Frédéric Dubois, Great Council Hall. Followed by a discussion and an informal gathering with refreshments. The remainder of the morning is reserved for this event.

13:30–15:00

Calculation workflow and Python preprocessing

Preprocessing, computation and post-processing; bodies, materials, contactors, interaction laws and command scripts in an existing example.

15:15–17:00

Guided 2D practical and parameter exploration

Set up and run a rigid-body example; vary geometry, friction or loading, and visualise and interpret the results.

03

Thursday, 3 December 2026

Contact mechanics & 3D rigid bodies

09:00–10:45

Contact mechanics and NSCD

Contact kinematics, separation, sticking, sliding and impacts; time integration and the coupled contact problem.

11:00–12:30

Detection, solution and interaction laws

Contact detection, the Nonlinear Gauss–Seidel solver, and frictional and cohesive interactions.

13:30–15:00

Guided 3D practical

Preprocess and run a rigid-body example; examine contact definitions, boundary conditions and contact reactions.

15:15–16:00

Results and numerical settings

Inspect motion, reactions and solver convergence; distinguish physical instability from numerical difficulties.

16:00–17:00

3D modelling exercises and validation checks

Extend the worked example, verify contact definitions and boundary conditions, and document the calculation settings.

04

Friday, 4 December 2026

Deformable bodies & masonry applications

09:00–09:45

Introduction to deformable models

Distinguish material behaviour within a body from interaction behaviour at its boundary.

09:45–10:45

Gmsh and mesh preparation

Supplied geometry, physical groups, mesh dimensions, element types, mesh size and export.

11:00–12:30

Guided mesh-based practical

Import a mesh, assign the mechanical model, supports and loading, and inspect displacement and stress outputs.

13:30–15:00

Masonry applications

Selected examples of cohesive interactions, joint parameters, gravity equilibrium, loading and experimental comparisons.

15:15–17:00

Guided masonry modelling practical

Apply the course workflow to a selected masonry example, check interface parameters and numerical settings, and interpret and document the results.
