LMGC90 Workshop

Modelling masonry structures

When

2–4 December 2026
09:00–17:00 daily

Where

Zagreb, Croatia
University of Zagreb
Faculty of Civil Engineering

Three days of theory and guided practice in LMGC90, with a focus on contact mechanics and the modelling of masonry structures.

The workshop moves from the basic calculation workflow and 2D rigid-body examples to contact mechanics, 3D models and selected masonry applications. Participants will work with existing tutorials and scripts, supported by the organising team.

Lecturer
Frédéric Dubois · CNRS / LMGC, Montpellier

Practical support
Maja Baniček & Mahmoud Shaqfa

Registration & enquiries ↗View programme ↓

Contact: fragments@grad.unizg.hr

Who it is for

Researchers, doctoral students and engineers.

Basic mechanics, introductory Python and Unix terminal skills are recommended. Previous knowledge of Non-Smooth Contact Dynamics (NSCD) or Cohesive Zone Models (CZM) is not required.

Participants should arrive with a working course installation. Software requirements and preparation details will be provided before the workshop.

Three days of training

Preliminary programme

The programme may be adjusted slightly by the lecturer. The teaching sequence combines theory, guided examples and practical support.

Daily breaks: 10:45–11:00 and 15:00–15:15. Lunch: 12:30–13:30.

02

Wednesday, 2 December 2026

LMGC90 workflow & 2D rigid bodies

09:00–09:30

Welcome and introduction

Participant interests, LMGC90 capabilities and selected masonry applications.

09:30–10:45

The calculation workflow

Preprocessing, computation and post-processing; the user environment, documentation and tutorials.

11:00–12:30

Python preprocessing

Bodies, materials, models, contactors, interaction laws, visibility tables and command scripts, using an existing example.

13:30–15:00

Guided 2D practical

Geometry, density, gravity, boundary conditions and contact definitions. Run a rigid-body example and inspect the output.

15:15–16:30

Explore model parameters

Modify geometry, friction or loading and interpret the effect through post-processing and plots.

16:30–17:00

Review and discussion

Questions, practical difficulties and the physical interpretation of a selected result.

03

Thursday, 3 December 2026

Contact mechanics & 3D rigid bodies

09:00–10:45

Contact mechanics and NSCD

Contact kinematics, reactions, separation, sticking, sliding and impacts; time integration and the coupled contact problem.

11:00–12:30

Detection, solution and interaction laws

Contact detection, the Nonlinear Gauss–Seidel solver, and frictional and cohesive interactions.

13:30–15:00

Guided 3D practical

Preprocess a rigid-body example, inspect contact definitions, apply boundary conditions and run the calculation.

15:15–16:30

Results and numerical settings

Examine motion, reactions and solver information. Compare a numerical parameter with a baseline where practical.

16:30–17:00

Convergence and practical support

Discuss computational efficiency and the distinction between physical instability and numerical difficulties.

04

Friday, 4 December 2026

Deformable bodies & masonry applications

09:00–09:45

Introduction to deformable models

Distinguish material behaviour within a body from interaction behaviour at its boundary.

09:45–10:45

Gmsh and mesh preparation

Supplied geometry, physical groups, mesh dimensions, element types, mesh size and export.

11:00–12:30

Guided mesh-based practical

Import a mesh, assign the mechanical model, supports and loading, and inspect displacement and stress outputs.

13:30–15:00

Masonry applications

Selected examples of cohesive interactions, joint parameters, gravity equilibrium, loading and experimental comparisons.

15:15–16:30

Practical support and discussion

Course examples, questions submitted in advance, contact definitions, scripts, numerical settings and interpretation.

16:30–17:00

Conclusions and next steps

Documentation, example resources and the next checks for participants’ own applications.
